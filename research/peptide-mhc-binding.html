FIRST PROJECT

Why study peptide–MHC binding?

MHC proteins can bind short peptide fragments and display them for immune surveillance. Which peptides bind depends in part on the MHC variant. That gives our first project a defined question: can feedback help choose which peptides to evaluate next?
